It stays on your machine

Your memories are stored and encrypted on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

Any assistant can read it

One memory, shared across the assistants you already use. Connect them once and they recall the same things.

You can delete all of it

One button destroys the encryption key and the files. What remains on disk becomes unreadable — to everyone, including us.

Version 0.2.0 · 64-bit · 205 MB · Windows 10 and 11
Download .msi

Two things will happen when you install it

Windows will say the publisher is unknown. We are a very small team and have not yet bought a code-signing certificate. Choose More info and then Run anyway. We would rather tell you this now than have you wonder.

You will be asked for administrator permission. Zaaheen installs for everyone on the computer, so Windows asks once. It does not need administrator rights to run afterwards.
